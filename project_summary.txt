Student Performance Prediction project comparing Logistic Regression, Support Vector Machine (RBF), and Gaussian Naive Bayes on the mathematics subset of the UCI Student Performance dataset. A three-level Performance label is derived from G3: Low (0–9), Medium (10–14), and High (15–20). Reported test-set accuracy: Logistic Regression 53.16%, SVM 49.37%, Gaussian Naive Bayes 26.58%. Reported stratified five-fold mean accuracy: SVM 55.05%, Logistic Regression 48.39%, Gaussian Naive Bayes 30.04%. See README and notebook for methodology, limitations, and detailed metrics. Results are reproduced from the supplied output files and were not independently rerun during repository preparation.
